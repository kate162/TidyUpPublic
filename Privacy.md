This privacy policy explains what information TidyUp ("the Game") collects, why, and what choices you have. The Game is developed and published by Pinkgrif ("we", "us"), an independent game developer based in Israel (pinkgrif.com).
By downloading or playing the Game, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.
Information we collect
We do not ask for your name, email address, or any account login. The Game has no sign-up. Your game progress is saved on your device.
Through the third-party services described below, the Game automatically collects:
• Device information: device model, operating system version, screen size, language, and app version
• Identifiers: your device's advertising ID (such as the Google Advertising ID) and a randomly generated install ID
• Approximate location: country or region, derived from your IP address (we do not collect precise GPS location)
• Gameplay data: sessions, levels or progress reached, in-game events, and play time
• Ad data: which ads were shown, clicked, or watched
• Diagnostics: crash logs and performance data
You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
Third-party services
The Game uses two third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy that governs how it handles data.
GameAnalytics helps us understand how players use the Game, so we can fix problems and improve it. It collects device information, identifiers, approximate location, gameplay data, and diagnostics. See the GameAnalytics Privacy Policy.
Google AdMob shows ads in the Game, which keeps the Game free. AdMob may use your advertising ID, IP address, and device information to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. Ads may be personalized based on your interests unless you opt out. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.
If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, the Game will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice from the Game's settings. Everywhere else, you can opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings.
How we use information
We use the information above only to run and improve the Game, fix bugs and crashes, understand which features players enjoy, and show ads. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone other than the services listed above.
Children
The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.
Data retention and security
Analytics and advertising data is kept by GameAnalytics and Google according to their own retention policies. Data saved on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game. We rely on these providers' security measures, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
Your rights
Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in Europe or privacy laws in California), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your data, or to object to its use. To make a request, contact us at the email below. Because we do not collect your name or email, we may ask for your device's advertising ID so we can find your data.
